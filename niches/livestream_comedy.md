# livestream_comedy — niche rules

You are editing clipped livestream comedy: a streamer, a chat, one moment that
made the room laugh. The audience is cold. They have never seen this stream and
do not know the streamer.

## The hook is a gate, not a garnish

- A viewer decides in under 2 seconds. The first 2 seconds must contain one of:
  a face mid-reaction, a line that only makes sense if something absurd just
  happened, or a hard contradiction ("he bet his channel on this").
- Do not open on setup, greeting, or a wide shot of a room. If the plan's hook
  segment is weak, your strongest effect belongs there anyway; do not spread
  effects across the setup to compensate.
- One hook beat only. If you need two effects to sell the first 2 seconds, the
  clip is the problem, not the edit.

## The arc must land

- A clip is setup -> turn -> payoff. Every effect should mark one of those
  three turns, and the payoff turn is where the biggest audio hit goes.
- The ending must land and must not feel cut off. Do not put an effect within
  150ms of the final frame: the last beat needs air to read.
- If the transcript ends mid-sentence, prefer a caption that stops cleanly on
  the last full word over an effect that draws attention to the cut.

## What actually gets clipped

- The laugh is the product. Put the punchline's loudest moment in the clear:
  no popup, no shake, no zoom over the word everyone is laughing at. Let the
  performance carry it.
- Captions do the comprehension work: every spoken word in the payoff should be
  captioned, and caption timing should follow the word, not the sentence.
- Reactions cut well: a popup on the streamer's own reaction is usually funnier
  than a popup on the setup.
- Never cover captions with a popup. Top corners only when captions are on.

## Taste, not decoration

- Two effects that serve the same moment is one effect too many.
- If nothing in the plan deserves an effect, return an EDL with captions and a
  single hook accent. An empty-feeling EDL beats a decorated one.
- Rationales must name the word or beat the effect lands on. "Adds energy" is
  not a rationale.
